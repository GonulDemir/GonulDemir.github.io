Gönül Demir

Research & Development Engineer | Hardware, Sensors & EMC

Download Resume (PDF)

I am an electronics R&D engineer with over 15 years of experience. I have designed electronic circuits and products for a range of applications, with a particular focus on sensor and optoelectronic systems. My work has covered prototype development, testing, certification and the transition to production. I have followed these products through years of serial production, resolving issues encountered in manufacturing and in the field.

This experience shapes my approach to design. Alongside achieving a working circuit in the laboratory, I consider manufacturability, verifiable performance and reliability under real operating conditions to be fundamental design goals.
