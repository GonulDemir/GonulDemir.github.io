Industrial robots share a single metal chassis between high-power motor drivers and sensitive sensors (encoders, IMUs, CAN-Bus). Isolating switching noise ($di/dt$) is critical to prevent system resets.

## 1. Common Mode Noise & Ground Loops

Ground potential differences $\Delta V_G$ create common mode noise:

$$V_{error} = I_{ground} \cdot Z_{ground}$$

Digital isolators (e.g. ADuM1200) or optocouplers effectively break ground loops.
